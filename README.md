**Full-stack AI product builder.** I pick problems worth solving, build real products for them, put them in front of real users, and ship, with AI coding agents as my primary tool.

I'm the founder of [Sundae](https://sundaeplanner.com), an AI-guided weekly planner, and I study product management at BYU's Marriott School of Business.

## What I'm building

- **[sundae](https://github.com/tyleramberry/sundae)** is an AI-guided weekly planning app organized by life roles.
- **[jarvis](https://github.com/tyleramberry/jarvis)** is a voice-first personal AI assistant for an always-on Mac Mini. It runs a local wake word and talks to a realtime voice model, with no Alexa and no ads.
- **[opencal](https://github.com/tyleramberry/opencal)** is free, open-source booking links, a TidyCal-style scheduler.

- **[edge-ai-system](https://github.com/tyleramberry/edge-ai-system)** is an agentic system build-out for Streak Advisors.
- **[CFO-agent](https://github.com/tyleramberry/CFO-agent)** is an AI finance agent, in progress.
- **[claude-skills](https://github.com/tyleramberry/claude-skills)** is my library of Claude Code and second-brain skills.
